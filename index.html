DNA Thermal Denaturation Lab

What Experiment Are We Running? As DNA is heated, heat breaks the hydrogen bonds holding base pairs together, unzipping the double helix into single strands. Single-stranded DNA absorbs significantly more UV light at 260 nm (A₂₆₀) than double-stranded DNA. By tracking absorbance across temperatures, you will map denaturation curves to discover each sample's melting temperature (Tm) and infer its G–C vs. A–T composition.
